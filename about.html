A student's bookshelf, shared

ANS Book Store began in a university hostel room, when our founder kept lending her own books to friends who couldn't afford new copies. The shelf grew into a store, and the lending habit stayed.

Today we stock novels, Islamic classics and study guides, and we still price every title with a student's budget in mind.
